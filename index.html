WHY IT MATTERS

Smells carry safety information.

01 Food spoilage

Problem: Spoilage can be missed in kitchens and supply chains.

How: An e-nose can flag a changing scent profile for review.

02 Gas and smoke

Problem: Early warning can be hard to notice in homes and hostels.

How: An e-nose can watch for unusual gas or smoke patterns.

03 Indoor air quality

Problem: Classrooms and workplaces may have stale or changing air.

How: An e-nose can screen patterns that deserve attention.

04 Accessible descriptions

Problem: People with anosmia cannot rely on their sense of smell.

How: An e-nose can translate scent patterns into words.
